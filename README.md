Honeybee Simulator 🐝🍯

Created by a real-life beekeeper from Coorg (Kodagu), Honeybee Simulator is a cozy beekeeping and farm management game where you build, breed, care for, and expand your own apiary. Gather materials, craft bee boxes, tend to your colonies, protect them from pests, and harvest golden honey at your own pace. Store your first honey harvest in the cupboard to unlock the debris-filled plot beside the front-left fence, then build the sideways-facing roadside shop. Walk-up villagers can earn you tips, reputation, and higher future payouts.

For a local preview, run `python3 -m http.server 8000` from this folder and open `http://localhost:8000/`. Some browsers block the soundtrack when the game is opened directly as a `file://` URL.

Move with WASD or the arrow keys, or drag the on-screen joystick on a touch device. Tap or click action prompts; wood and scrap are collected automatically when you walk nearby. Browser pinch zoom is supported.
